WELCOME TO SACRAMENTO

Where science meets Sacramento.

Welcome to Sacramento, California's vibrant capital city and the host destination for the 2027 ASAS-CSAS-WSASAS Annual Meeting.

Gather at the SAFE Credit Union Convention Center from June 27 to July 1 for scientific exchange, collaboration, networking, and professional development.

Between sessions, experience Sacramento's Farm-to-Fork culinary scene, shaded downtown streets, historic Old Sacramento Waterfront, California State Capitol, and nearby riverfront attractions.
